Evidence-first vehicle care

Maintenance records you can prove.

AutoVault is a self-hosted application for recording vehicle service and preserving original photos, receipts, invoices, parts, fluids, costs, and warranty evidence.

Private home serverSHA-256 integrityVerified Drive copy
01

Capture

Log DIY or professional maintenance and upload the untouched original evidence from a phone or computer.

02

Verify

AutoVault calculates a SHA-256 hash and verifies the original file stored on the owner’s home server.

03

Protect

A background service creates a second copy in Google Drive and verifies it by downloading and hashing it again.

Narrow access by design

Google Drive is used only for AutoVault backups.

AutoVault requests the non-sensitive drive.file scope. It can create and manage only the Drive files it creates or that the user explicitly makes available to it; it does not request broad access to unrelated Drive content. Read the privacy policy for full data-use and retention details.
